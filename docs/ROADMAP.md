# Delivery roadmap

1. **Data foundation:** instrument master ingestion, contract identity, quote snapshots, WebSocket lifecycle, persistence and quality checks.
2. **Options analytics:** option-chain snapshots, IV and Greeks with explicit model assumptions, expiry/strike selection.
3. **Research:** market regime, signals, strategy registry, calibrated probability evaluation.
4. **Backtesting:** contract-level data, costs, spread/slippage, walk-forward and out-of-sample evaluation.
5. **Paper execution:** virtual ledger, conservative fills, partial-fill model, exits, risk circuit breakers and audit journal.
6. **Dashboard:** market context, ranked candidates, positions, P&L, decision journal and reports.
7. **Forward validation:** multiple market regimes, sample-size review, reconciliation and independent readiness assessment.
8. **Live readiness:** separate project phase requiring regulatory/broker verification and explicit user approval; not part of this scaffold.
